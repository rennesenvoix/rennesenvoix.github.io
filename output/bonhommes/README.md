## Consignes de génération

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied original Rennes en Voix festival choir artwork. Extract ONLY the specified existing character as a standalone transparent PNG asset. Preserve faithfully its thin near-black outlines, white filled circular head and rounded simple bust, closed curved eyelids, large O singing mouth, original dry-brush texture and exact saturated colors. No redesign, no shading, no text, no added limbs, no other characters or their marks, no checkerboard baked in. True transparent background outside the figure; keep white inside face/body. Reconstruct only parts of the bust concealed by the other singers with the same simple rounded outline. Center entire isolated character with breathing room. 

1. Frontmost singer: turquoise long hair and magenta polka dots. Remove adjacent orange flower.

2. Top left singer: orange hair buns and turquoise vertical stripes. Remove neighboring black spikes.

3. Top right singer: purple spiky tuft and plain white rounded bust.
